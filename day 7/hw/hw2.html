My Favorite Film: A Bronx Tale

About the Movie

A Bronx Tale is a 1993 crime drama film directed by Robert De Niro. The movie is set in the 1960s in the Bronx, New York. It is based on a true story written by Chazz Palminteri, who also plays one of the main characters in the film.

The Story

The story follows a young Italian-American boy named Calogero. When he was a child, he witnesses a murder committed by a local gangster named Sonny. Instead of telling the police the truth, Calogero protects Sonny. Because of this, Sonny starts to take Calogero under his wing and becomes like a second father to him.

Calogero grows up feeling torn between two different role models. His real father, Lorenzo, is an honest and hardworking bus driver who believes in staying out of trouble and earning money the right way. Sonny, on the other hand, is powerful, respected, and involved in crime. Calogero must decide which path he wants to follow.

Main Characters

- Calogero: The main character who grows up in the Bronx.
- Lorenzo: Calogero's father, who teaches him strong moral values.
- Sonny: The local mob boss who becomes an important influence in Calogero’s life.

Themes and Message

The movie teaches important lessons about family, respect, loyalty, racism, and making the right choices in life. It shows how difficult it can be to grow up and choose between right and wrong.
